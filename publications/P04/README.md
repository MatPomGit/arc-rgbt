# P04: Failure-Aware RGB–Thermal Detection

**Classification:** CORE — planned. **Date:** 2026-10-28.
This is a proposed publication, not a completed manuscript or a claim of empirical performance.

**Research question.** Does explicit modality-failure monitoring and selective prediction lower missed detections under missing/degraded RGB or LWIR at matched false-positive and minimum-coverage budgets?

**Independent contribution.** P04 measures risk under failure, not average P02 fusion mAP. Merge into P02 if no defensible independent risk outcome is demonstrated.
