# Literature screening queue

The [MkDocs literature triage](../../docs/experiments/literature-screening.md)
indexes a **selected, deduplicated subset** of 98 imported bibliographic
records. It does not copy the original document or bibliography export
into this public repository.

## Provenance and boundaries

- Intake reviewed: 2026-10-27, owner-supplied historical RGB/thermal
  and aerial imaging documents plus an exported bibliography.
- 98 source rows, 91 case-normalized distinct titles, 75 records with a DOI,
  72 distinct DOI strings, 23 missing DOI values.
- The shortlist is **not systematic**. Original PDF text, abstracts,
  unpublished commercial schedules, vendor/component price lists,
  operational flight plans and autonomous pursuit instructions are excluded.
- Four publisher record identities were checked. Other candidate
  bibliographic strings remain **metadata-only**, not validated citations.
- Imported performance claims must be verified on the primary publication
  before being used in scientific argument or experiment acceptance criteria.

Use [evaluation requirements](../../docs/guides/evaluation-requirements.md)
for protocol design. Store future evidence with an exact DOI, dataset
licence, sensor grid and method-specific metric definition; do not treat
synthetic E00/E01/E02 output as physical experiment results.
